Project Revision

While creating this website, I faced many difficulties. Overall, it was challenging to manage the coding, the direction of the design, and maintaining a consistent tone and atmosphere throughout the project.

Among these, the most difficult part was building the demo page. Even though it may seem simple, I had to create everything from the game engine to the objects, background, spawn positions, and interface, which made it quite demanding.

There were issues like mismatched coordinates between the screen, the mouse, and the website itself. Sometimes the AI misunderstood my requests and produced strange results. There were also errors that appeared for no clear reason, and I even remember making my own debugging tools because of that.

However, despite all these difficulties, there was a certain thrill— sitting at my desk, struggling for hours, and finally seeing the code work exactly the way I wanted. That feeling was so rewarding that I couldn’t stop (seriously).

So yes, I think this project came together through both the challenges and the sense of reward—almost like the dopamine you get from solving problems.

Thank you for reading.
