About Zont

Welcome to Zont. We do not build tanks. We do not build infantry fighting vehicles. We build the definitive guarantee that the soldiers inside them come home alive.

Headquartered in the industrial heartland of Eastern Europe, Zont is a premier defense technology firm specializing exclusively in next-generation Active Protection Systems (APS).

Our Story: The Latecomer’s Advantage

Zont was not born out of a bloated, Cold War-era state conglomerate. We are a distinctly modern enterprise, founded by visionary engineers Hye Kang Jee and D.Y. Kim.

Before founding Zont, Jee and Kim spent years analyzing global armored conflicts. They recognized a fatal flaw in the legacy defense industry: the pioneers of APS technology were trapped by their own outdated architectures.

We didn't invent the Active Protection System. We perfected it.

Efficiency Through Intelligence

As a latecomer, Zont entered the field unburdened by legacy code. Instead, Jee and Kim leveraged the greatest asset of the 21st century: Data. By analyzing thousands of terabytes of combat telemetry, Zont developed algorithmic models that predict and intercept threats with unprecedented efficiency.

Traditional doctrine favored brute force. We evolved that. While our hardware is built to withstand the harshest environments, our software operates with surgical elegance. By utilizing advanced AI, our systems classify and eliminate threats using the exact minimum kinetic force required, radically reducing collateral damage for dismounted infantry.

Our Flagship: The Zontik-1 APS

The culmination of our data-centric approach is our flagship product: the Zontik-1 ("Little Umbrella"). It is a highly aggressive, 360-degree kinetic shield utilizing miniaturized AESA radars and hyper-fast electro-optical sensors.

The Zontik-1 boasts the highest threat-discrimination rate in the industry. It will not waste countermeasures on non-threats. When a genuine threat is detected, it deploys a tightly focused, high-explosive kinetic interceptor to physically shatter the incoming projectile before it ever touches the hull.

The Future is Covered

Warfare is changing, and the threats against armor are becoming smarter. But under the leadership of Hye Kang Jee and D.Y. Kim, Zont remains exactly one step ahead. We are continuously feeding new battlefield data into our neural networks, ensuring that our systems evolve faster than the weapons designed to beat them.

When the skies rain steel, we keep you covered.
