About Us

Modular Architecture

The system is built around an independent, dual-tube rotary turret that can be seamlessly integrated onto diverse vehicular platforms.

  • 360-degree Azimuth & -15° to +75° Elevation coverage.
  • Dual Interceptor Launch Tubes for rapid sequential engagement.
  • Standalone power unit with independent logic controller.

Externally Mounted

Built as a stand-alone bolt-on solution to provide kinetic protection with minimum impact on the base vehicle structure.

  • Preserves the integrity of the vehicle’s primary armor.
  • Easily integrated without complex structural modifications.
  • Ideally suited for both new production and retrofitting existing fleets.
