System Overview

The Zontik-1 is a modular, externally mounted Active Protection System designed to provide survivability without the structural penalties of legacy systems. Built around an independent, dual-axis rotary turret, the system can be integrated onto any armored platform—from Main Battle Tanks (MBTs) to lightweight Infantry Fighting Vehicles (IFVs).

By combining robust hardware engineering with proprietary, data-driven threat discrimination software, the Zontik-1 offers a 360-degree kinetic defense.

System Architecture Externally mounted dual-tube rotary turret (Standalone)
Total System Weight Under 450 kg (Configuration dependent)
Reaction Time < 250 milliseconds
Protection Arc 360° Azimuth / -15° to +75° Elevation
Sensor Suite 4x Distributed Miniaturized AESA Radars + Integrated EO/IR Cameras
Interceptor Type Fin-stabilized, high-explosive blast interceptor
Threats Defeated ATGMs, RPGs, Recoilless Rifles, HEAT, Loitering Munitions
Operating Temp. -50°C to +65°C
